HALF SWORD SAVE RECOVERY (WINDOWS)

Use this tool to transfer selected progress from an old Half Sword save to a fresh one. It works directly on .sav files with Windows PowerShell. UeSaveConverter, JSON, and extra downloads are unnecessary.

HOW TO RESTORE

1. Keep a separate copy of your old save. Start a new game, play a quick battle, then quit Half Sword so it finishes saving.
2. Put Restore Half Sword Save.bat, a copy of your old save, and a copy of the fresh save in the same folder. Use different filenames for the two saves. You can find the game's save at:
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\HalfSwordUE5\Saved\SaveGames\GameProgress.sav
3. Double-click Restore Half Sword Save.bat.
   Restore FROM: Enter the old save filename, or press Enter for gsc.sav.
   Restore TO: Enter the fresh save filename, or press Enter for GameProgress.sav.
   Enter filenames including .sav. Both files must be beside the batch file.
4. Enter the total gold you want, or press Enter for 12,000.
5. Wait for the verification message. The tool updates the selected destination and keeps a timestamped copy of its prior contents in the same folder. The backup filename starts with the destination name followed by " - before restore ".
6. With Half Sword closed, back up the live save, then copy the restored destination into the game's SaveGames folder as GameProgress.sav. Launch the game and check your progress and insured gear at the trader.

WHAT GETS TRANSFERRED

- Height, weight, and fat
- Day count and Baron defeats
- Player tier, permanent tier, and rank reputation
- The full Insured Items section, including insured armor and weapons

The source insurance list replaces the destination insurance list. The destination keeps its character name, equipped gear, and regular inventory. Use the trader to access restored insured gear. The gold prompt sets the total Player Funds.

TO UNDO

Use the timestamped pre-restore backup to roll back the destination file. If you already installed the restored save in the game, quit Half Sword and put your live-save backup back as GameProgress.sav.

TO SHARE

Share only the batch file and this guide. Keep your .sav files and backups private.
